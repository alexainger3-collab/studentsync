Auto-balance your school week around classes, sport, sleep and study time.

## Long description

StudentSync builds your weekly schedule around what's already fixed — classes,
work shifts, sport — and automatically fills the gaps with sleep and
independent study, so you always know what's next and how much free time you
actually have.

**What it does:**
- Add your classes, work shifts, and recurring sports — StudentSync builds
  the rest of your week around them.
- Automatic independent-study scheduling that fits into your real free time,
  and pauses itself during holidays.
- External activities and supercurricular time (wider reading, enrichment),
  either weekly or one-off.
- A scheduling assistant that finds a free slot for something new and lets
  you confirm before it's added — nothing is scheduled without you approving it.
- Ask about your schedule in plain language: "what's on Monday?", "how much
  sport this week?", "when's my next holiday?"
- Tick off completed sessions and log actual sleep, then see how the week
  and month compare against what was planned.
